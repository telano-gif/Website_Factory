# Research Skill

## Purpose
Turn the approved intake into a verified website-relevant knowledge base.

## Input
`INTAKE.md` plus supplied source material.

## Produce
`RESEARCH.md` containing:
- verified company facts
- audience/context relevant to the site
- offerings and differentiators supported by evidence
- useful industry context
- factual gaps requiring client input
- source/evidence notes for consequential claims
- website implications

## Rules
- Research only what can improve the website.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources.
- Never convert inference into fact.
- Flag geographic, temporal or source mismatches.
- Do not invent differentiators, statistics, testimonials or credentials.
- Do not prescribe visual design; Design consumes the research.

## Complete when
The design/content stages have enough verified context to make informed decisions without unsupported claims.

## Handoff
`RESEARCH_COMPLETE` → Design consumes `INTAKE.md` + `RESEARCH.md`.
